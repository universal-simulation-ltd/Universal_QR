# Universal QR — translation glossary

Every translator of this app uses these terms, in the app (`src/i18n/<lang>/`)
and in the store text, so the app, its knowledge base (`src/knowledge/<lang>.ts`,
translated first — these terms are taken from it) and its store listing say
the same thing.

English (en-GB) is the source. If a term is missing, add it here first, then use it.
UI labels are in sentence case in every language (only the first word and proper
nouns get a capital; German nouns keep theirs). Universal PDF's glossary
(`Universal_Apps/Universal_PDF/src/i18n/GLOSSARY.md`) covers the shared suite
words — file, save, download, share, sign in — and wins where both have a term.

## Never translated

UNI·SIM, UNISIM.co.uk, Universal ID, Universal QR, Universal PDF, GitHub, Wi-Fi,
SSID, SMS (Turkish may write SMS), PNG, SVG, JPEG, WebP, EAN-13, UPC-A, Code 128,
Code 39, ITF-14, and every QR payload (`WIFI:`, `mailto:`, `BEGIN:VCARD`…).

## Register and style

Match the knowledge base and Universal PDF: **vous** in French, **tú** in
Spanish, **tu** in Italian, **du** (lower case) in German, **você** (usually
implied) in pt-BR, the infinitive/impersonal in pt-PT (avoid *tu* and *você*),
and Turkish without spelling out *siz* where the sentence works without it.
PDF's glossary has the full per-language notes (button forms, punctuation such
as the French space before ? and !) — follow them.

## The headline

"QR codes that {em}. Forever." with `{em}` = "just work", highlighted. Keep the
highlighted part a short phrase that reads as the payoff, and "Forever." a
separate short sentence after it.
